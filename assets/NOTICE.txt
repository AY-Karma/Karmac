The Karmac signal-K app icon is original project artwork, generated from
geometric paths by tools/Create-AppIcon.py (Pillow). Karmac.svg is the editable
vector export; Karmac.png and Karmac.ico are rendered exports. No third-party
logos, fonts or stock icon files are incorporated. See docs/branding.md for
design references. These project-authored assets use the project's MIT license.

The embedded AirPods Pro 3 illustration was originally generated for Karmac.
The AirPods, AirPods Pro, AirPods Max, Beats Studio, Beats Solo, Beats buds,
Beats Fit Pro, Powerbeats Pro, Beats neckband and cabled Powerbeats family renders
in assets/headphones are original AI-generated product illustrations
created with the built-in imagegen tool for Karmac. They show example
finishes and approximate family design, not exact color or generation identity.
They are embedded in the executable and do not require downloads at runtime.
These are illustrative assets, not official Apple product renders. Their generated
alpha and soft edge light are preserved. The original vector illustrations in
MainWindow.xaml remain available while raster artwork loads or is unavailable.

Model-specific local images supplied by the user are loaded from the user's
Karmac/Artwork folder and are not embedded or included in release archives.
Windows manufacturer device images, when available, are retrieved for the paired
device only. Their availability and authorship are not guaranteed.
Apple, AirPods and Beats product names identify compatible products. Karmac
is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple Inc.
