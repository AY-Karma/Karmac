Family design and finish are illustrative, not exact per-generation or detected color. Generated transparency is preserved. Local licensed user images retain priority. No Apple marketing photographs were downloaded or redistributed.

## Generation prompts

### max.png

Use case: product-mockup. Create a new original premium photorealistic studio 3D product illustration of silver AirPods Max headphones, not a vector icon. One single pair only, entire product visible, 3/4 front angle turned slightly left with nearer aluminum outer earcup visible on right of image, paired oval rounded rectangular silver anodized aluminum shells, pale gray deep fabric mesh ear cushions, slim polished telescoping stainless steel yokes, distinctive thin inverted U headband surrounding broad breathable pale grey knit mesh canopy, small precise digital crown and button atop right cup. Accurate recognizable AirPods Max silhouette. Floating upright product isolated on actual transparent alpha background, no floor, no shadow plane, no case, no wires, no labels or text. Beautiful controlled studio softbox light, detailed realistic machined metal and woven fabric, subtle depth, crisp antialiased edges, bright enough for pure black app background. Product occupies 85 percent of square frame with generous safe margins. High resolution.
